# Report Navigator

**What information do you need?**  

- **A quick executive snapshot?**  
  Go to the **Executive Summary** for the top-line takeaways and key metrics.

- **Full transparency on inputs & methods?**  
  Open **Study Info** for product specs, LCA choices, scenarios and boundaries.

- **Spot the biggest hotspots fast?**  
  Check **Summary** for normalized impacts, Sankey flows and bar charts.

- **Benchmark the overall system?**  
  Select **System Analysis** for stage-level bars, sunburst and element contributions.

- **Dive into subsystems?**  
  Use **Assembly Analysis** to compare assembly-level impacts and material/process breakdowns.

- **Inspect individual parts?**  
  Head to **Component Analysis** for part-specific impacts and process contributions.

- **Compare design alternatives?**  
  Visit **Design change comparison** to see variant vs baseline results.

*(Click any tab at the top to jump straight to that section.)*
