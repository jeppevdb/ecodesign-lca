## Summary

This tab provides a consolidated view of the results across all assemblies and scenarios. Use this tab to get a more detailed overview of the product's environmental impacts and to identify areas of interest to investigate further.


   **Charts:**

   - Sankey diagram showing the flow of impacts throughout the system  
   - Ranked bar chart showing the most relevant assemblies categories
   - Sunburst diagram showing a hierarchical breakdown of impacts and uncertainty levels
   - Tornado plot showing the system's sensitivity to individual scenario parameters being changed
