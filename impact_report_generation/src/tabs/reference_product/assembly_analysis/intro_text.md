## Assembly-Level Analysis

Drill into a single assembly to see its internal breakdown and sensitivities. You can choose any one subsystem from the overall product and compare how its components and impact categories vary by scenario and region.  

### Charts:  
- Three ranked bar charts showing the most relevant components within the selected assembly, broken down by process contribution & scenario and country-based variation
- Ranked bar chart of the most relevant impact categories for that assembly  
- Assembly-level sunbursts comparing uncertainty across countries and scenarios  
- Tornado plot showing a assembly’s sensitivity to changes in individual scenario parameters  

### Global assembly selector
- Select a specific assembly with the global selector (shown below) to set all of the remaining tabs to show results for the selected assembly. Within each of the tabs it is still possible to select another assembly using the local override selector.
